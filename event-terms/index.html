5. Code of Conduct

We want everyone to feel comfortable and safe. By attending you agree to:

  • Treat every guest, our host, the venue staff and the tarot reader with respect and courtesy.
  • Respect personal space. Do not touch anyone without their clear consent, and accept "no" the first time.
  • Not harass, follow, intimidate or pester anyone, and not make unwelcome sexual, personal or discriminatory comments or jokes.
  • Not discriminate against anyone because of their age, sex, race, religion, sexual orientation, disability or any other protected characteristic.
  • Not photograph or record other guests without their permission, and not share other guests' personal details or social media accounts without their agreement.
  • Not use the evening to sell, promote or recruit for a business, or to ask guests for money.
  • Drink responsibly. Illegal drugs are not allowed.
  • Follow the venue's rules and the instructions of our host and the venue staff.

If anyone makes you feel uncomfortable or unsafe, please tell Anna or a member of the venue staff straight away and we will help.
