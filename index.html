BEING SINGLE IS HARDER THAN IT LOOKS

It has never been harder to meet your person.

Being single is not only about not having a partner. Somewhere along the way, friends paired off, moved away, started families or simply got busy. The spontaneous plans quietly stopped.

Whether you want a partner, a friend, or just someone to say yes to the next plan, finding your people has become harder than it should be.

The person behind every match and event

Matchmaking my friends was the start.Sparked Connection is where it led.

Anna Founder & Lead Matchmaker

I've always been the friend who introduces people to each other, and I've been matchmaking friends for a long time. Working in the corporate world, I met so many amazing people, and I kept noticing how many of them were single and struggling to find a partner. So I set up Sparked Connection.

I keep seeing the same thing. People who have built successful careers feel stuck when it comes to their personal lives. They are trying, but dating apps don't work for everyone, and for many the experience has become exhausting and discouraging. Meeting through friends used to be the other way in, but everyone is busy now, friends have moved on, and hardly anyone introduces people anymore.

I do two things: one-to-one matchmaking, and events like the ones I used to organise for my friends. I make them fun, the kind you would want to come to anyway. You meet other singles who want the same things you do, whether that's a partner or a friend, and afterwards I do the matching myself, which you will not find at most singles events.

It matters to me to grow the pool of available singles, so joining the Singles Club is completely free. Free to join and free to belong. Everyone I bring in is vetted, and being kind and respectful is non-negotiable.

GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you get started. Can't find your answer? Reach out and we'll help directly.

How does the matching process work? +

It starts with a private, one-to-one consultation so we can understand your values, lifestyle, and what a meaningful relationship looks like to you. From there, we draw on compatibility science and personal insight from your matchmaker to introduce you to people we believe are genuinely right for you - not just a list of profiles to swipe through.

What membership tiers are available, and what do they cost? +

Bronze is free and includes access to our Singles Network. Gold (£2,000, 12 months) includes 1:1 consultations, a bespoke search, dating coaching, VIP event access, and a professional photoshoot, with a guaranteed minimum of 5 curated introductions. Platinum (£3,800) gives two people - you and a friend - the full Gold experience each, with priority matching and a dedicated matchmaker.

Is there a guarantee on introductions? +

Gold and Platinum members receive a guaranteed minimum of 5 curated introductions over their 12-month membership, subject to eligibility, responsiveness, and active participation. If the minimum isn't met within the period, your membership is extended at no extra cost.

What is the Second Match Promise? +

It's an optional £150 add-on available when two Bronze members are matched with one another - only one person needs to pay. It guarantees a second introduction if the first match isn't the right fit, with no fixed time limit.

Can I come to events without a paid membership? +

Yes - our events are open to members across all tiers, with some events ticketed separately from membership fees. Keep an eye on the Events section on this page for what's coming up next.

Is my information kept private? +

Yes. Your details are handled discreetly and are never shared without your consent. Matchmaking is a personal process, and confidentiality is core to how we work.

What's your refund and cancellation policy? +

Bronze is free, so no refund applies. Gold, Platinum, and Second Match Promise fees are payable in advance and follow our full Refund & Cancellation Policy, which covers cooling-off periods, exceptional circumstances, and your statutory rights. Get in touch and we'll talk you through it.

How does Refer a Friend work? +

If you know someone who'd be a great fit for Sparked Connection, send them our way. Visit the Refer a Friend page for the details on how it works.
